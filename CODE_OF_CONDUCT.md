# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the HOMA project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming — especially to first-time contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Writing in clear, simple language (English or Persian both welcome)

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing automation intended to defraud, spam, cheat, or break other people's systems
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported to the maintainer through GitHub. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
